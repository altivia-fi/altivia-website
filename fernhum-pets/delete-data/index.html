Delete your Fernhum Pets data

For Fernhum Pets: Calming Sounds, published by Altivia Oy (business ID 3548452-7), Finland.

Fernhum Pets has no accounts, and we never receive what you save in it. Most of your data is on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time. The steps for each kind of data are below.

1. What you save in the app (on your phone)

  • One pet: open Profile, choose the pet, and tap Remove. Their profile and training history are deleted.
  • Everything: uninstall Fernhum Pets. This deletes all of its data from your phone. A copy may remain in your own iCloud or Google backup until that backup is replaced or deleted.

2. Subscription data (Fernhum Pets Premium)

If you buy Fernhum Pets Premium, RevenueCat, Inc. keeps your purchase status under a random, anonymous ID that is not linked to your name or email. Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your name, email, or card.

To request deletion, email contact@altivia.fi with the subject "Delete my Fernhum Pets data" and include the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt, so we can find the record. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept: transaction records that the law requires for tax and accounting are kept for as long as that law requires, and then deleted. Cancelling your subscription does not delete data; manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account.
