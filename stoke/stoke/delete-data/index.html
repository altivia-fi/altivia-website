Delete your Stoke data

For Stoke: Interval Timer, published by Altivia Apps (Altivia Oy, business ID 3548452-7, Finland).

Stoke has no accounts, and we never receive your workouts, history, or settings. Most of your data is on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time. The steps for each kind of data are below.

1. Workouts, history, and settings (on your phone)

  • One session: open History, tap the session, and delete it.
  • All history: open History and tap Clear.
  • A backup file: if you made one with Stoke Pro, it's wherever you saved or sent it; delete it there. Stoke and Altivia never have a copy.
  • A workout you made: open Workouts, tap the workout, and tap Delete.
  • Everything: uninstall Stoke. This deletes all of its data from your phone. A copy may remain in your own Google or iCloud backup until that backup is replaced or deleted.

2. Advertising data (free version)

When the free version shows ads, they come from Google AdMob, which receives device and usage information such as your advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads.

  • Change consent: in Stoke, open Settings, then Privacy choices (shown where the law requires it).
  • Android: open your phone's Settings, then Google, then Ads, and delete or reset your advertising ID. iPhone: turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • To ask Google to delete ad data, see how Google uses information from ads, or email us and we will help.

3. Subscription data (Stoke Pro)

If you buy Stoke Pro, RevenueCat, Inc. keeps your Pro status under a random, anonymous ID that is not linked to your name or email. Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your name, email, or card.

  • To request deletion, email contact@altivia.fi with the subject "Delete my Stoke data" and include the order number from your Google Play or App Store receipt (it starts with GPA. on Google Play), so we can find the record.
  • We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • What is kept: transaction records that the law requires for tax and accounting are kept for as long as that law requires, and then deleted. Cancelling your subscription does not delete data; manage or cancel it in your Google Play or App Store account.
