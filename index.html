How we work

From idea to launch

Clear steps, working software early, and no surprises.

  1. Discover

    We learn your goals, users, and existing systems, and agree on scope, timeline, and cost.

  2. Design & build

    Short iterations with a working version to try at every step, so you can steer as we go.

  3. Launch

    Store submission, hosting, and the checks that matter: accessibility, privacy, and performance.

  4. Support

    Updates, monitoring, and new features once real users arrive.
