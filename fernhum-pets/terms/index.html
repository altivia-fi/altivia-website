Using sound safely

Keep the volume low and supervise Sound Training. Stop right away if your pet looks distressed and let them move away from the sound. Use training when your pet is relaxed. Fernhum Pets is not veterinary advice. Ask a veterinarian or qualified behaviour professional about persistent fear or distress.
