Fernhum Pets

Calming sounds for dogs and cats.

Calming music and sound for fireworks and storms, time home alone, bedtime, and trips. Sound Training helps your dog or cat get used to fireworks, thunder, and the doorbell, a little at a time, and keeps a record of how they react.

What you get

  • Sound for the momentFireworks & Storms, Home Alone, Sleep, and Travel & Vet.
  • Music for dogs and catsSlow piano for dogs, and gentle music with a live purr for cats.

  • Every pet in your homeA profile for each pet, each with its own training levels and history.
