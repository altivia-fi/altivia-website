Fernhum

Sound for sleep, focus, and quiet moments.

Timed sound sessions for falling asleep, a power nap, deep work, winding down, and quiet moments. The sound changes as each session goes on, and you can set the mix of tones, noise, and nature sounds yourself.

Coming soon to the App Store Coming soon to Google Play

What you get

  • Sessions with a goalFall asleep, power nap, deep work, wind down, calm before, morning, and meditate.
  • Sound that moves with youEach session shifts its tones and textures as it goes, then ends gently.
  • Your own mixSet tones, noise, and backgrounds like rain, surf, wind, or a fire.
  • Quick tonesOne steady tone for a short listening break.
  • SchedulingSet a session for later. Keep Fernhum open, and allow notifications for a reminder.
  • No account neededEverything stays on your phone. Works offline.

Fernhum Premium

Fernhum is a paid app. Choose a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or a monthly subscription. Your app store shows the price and renewal terms before you confirm. Manage or cancel a subscription any time in your App Store or Google Play account.
