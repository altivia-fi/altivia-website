Fernhum Pets Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Fernhum Pets is a calming sound app for dogs and cats published by Altivia Oy (business ID 3548452-7) ("Altivia", "we", "us"), Finland. This policy explains what information Fernhum Pets uses, why, who receives it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Fernhum Pets works without an account. Your profile, choices, and history stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • Fernhum Pets has no ads, no analytics, and no crash-reporting tools.
  • Apple or Google handle payment for your subscription, and RevenueCat processes your subscription status using an anonymous ID.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information that stays on your device

Fernhum Pets stores the following only on your phone:

  • A profile for each pet you add: dog or cat, name, and the sounds that upset them.
  • Each pet's Sound Training: the level for each sound, the sound in use, and a dated history of their reactions.
  • The length, background, and other options you last chose for each situation.
  • Your Premium status, so the app works offline.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Removing Fernhum Pets deletes this data, except for any copy kept in your own device backup (iCloud or Google backup), which is governed by Apple's or Google's terms.

Device features Fernhum Pets uses

These work on your device and do not send your information to us:

  • Sound and background audio, so music keeps playing while the screen is locked, with playback controls on the lock screen. On Android this shows a "playing" notification while sound plays. Sound Training pauses as soon as you leave the app.

Fernhum Pets never uses the microphone. Its audio engine includes recording features that the app does not turn on, so your phone may list a microphone purpose, but Fernhum Pets never asks for access.

Subscriptions (Fernhum Pets Premium)

Fernhum Pets is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google process your payment. We never see your name, email, or payment card.

To check and restore your purchase, Fernhum Pets uses RevenueCat, Inc. (United States). RevenueCat receives:

  • A random, anonymous app user ID created by Fernhum Pets. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Purchase and subscription details from Apple or Google, such as product, price, currency, dates, trial and renewal status, and the country of the store account.
  • Basic device and app information, such as operating system, app version, and IP address.

This is needed to provide what you bought, so the legal basis is performance of a contract. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you choose to include. We use it to answer you, based on our legitimate interest in supporting the app, and keep it as long as needed to handle your request and meet legal obligations.

International transfers

RevenueCat may process information outside the EEA, including in the United States. These transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long information is kept

  • Information on your device: until you delete it or remove the app.
  • Subscription records: kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide your purchase and to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to handle your request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its use, and to data portability.

Because Fernhum Pets has no account and we do not store your data, most of your information is already under your control on your device. For subscription information, contact us and we will help with requests to RevenueCat. How to delete each kind of information is explained at altivia.fi/fernhum-pets/delete-data.

If you are in the EEA, you can also complain to a data protection authority. Ours is the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman. You may also contact the authority where you live.

Children

Fernhum Pets is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and UK. If you believe a child has shared personal information through Fernhum Pets, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and publish the new version before the change takes effect in the app. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
