Stoke Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Stoke is an interval timer published by Altivia Oy (business ID 3548452-7) ("Altivia", "we", "us"), Finland. This policy explains what information Stoke uses, why, who receives it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Stoke works without an account. Your workouts, history, and settings stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • The free version of Stoke may show banner and full-screen ads provided by Google AdMob. When it does, Google receives device and usage information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • If you subscribe to Stoke Pro, there are no ads. Apple or Google handle the payment, and RevenueCat processes your subscription status using an anonymous ID.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and Stoke has no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own.

Information that stays on your device

Stoke stores the following only in its local database on your phone:

  • Workouts you create, including names, timings, and chosen artwork.
  • Workout history: when you trained, which workout, how long, and whether you finished.
  • Settings, such as sounds, voice, vibration, and lock-screen alerts.
  • When the last backup was made, if you use Backup.
  • Your Stoke Pro status, so the app works offline.
  • Recovery data for a workout in progress, so a running workout survives the app closing.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

With Stoke Pro you can make a backup: a file with your workouts, history, and settings that Stoke creates on your phone and hands to the share sheet. It goes only where you choose to save or send it, such as your files, your own cloud storage, or email, and never to us. Restoring reads a backup file you pick and adds its contents on that phone. You can delete sessions, clear all history, and delete workouts in the app. Removing Stoke deletes this data, except for any copy kept in your own device backup (iCloud or Google backup), which is governed by Apple's or Google's terms.

Device features Stoke uses

Stoke uses these features to run your workout. They work on your device and do not send your workout information to us:

  • Sound, voice, and vibration for workout cues. Spoken cues use your device's built-in text-to-speech.
  • Background audio (iPhone) and a "workout running" notification (Android) to keep cues on time while the screen is locked. They stop when the workout ends.
  • Lock-screen progress: a Live Activity on iPhone and an ongoing notification on Android, showing the current exercise and countdown.
  • Notifications (optional): if you turn on lock-screen alerts, Stoke asks for notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device.

Advertising (free version)

When ads are on, the free version of Stoke shows banner ads on the Home, History, and workout-complete screens, and full-screen ads after you save a workout and before a workout starts, never during a workout. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, operated by Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, and by Google LLC elsewhere.

When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system, app version, language, and screen size.
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location (city or region, not precise GPS).
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Android advertising ID), only where permitted. See your choices below.
  • Ad interactions, such as ads shown and taps, and diagnostic information.

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure them, prevent fraud, and, if you allow it, personalize ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: before any ad loads, Stoke shows Google's consent message, where you can accept, reject, or choose purposes. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy choices.
  • iPhone: Stoke asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalized based on it.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.
  • United States: where state privacy laws give you the right to opt out of targeted advertising or the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, you can do so in Settings > Privacy choices.
  • Stoke Pro removes all ads, so no ad requests are made.

The legal basis for personalized ads in the EEA and UK is your consent. Non-personalized ads rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free app, together with consent for storing and reading information on your device where the law requires it.

Subscriptions (Stoke Pro)

Stoke Pro is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google process your payment. We never see your name, email, or payment card.

To check and restore your subscription, Stoke uses RevenueCat, Inc. (United States). RevenueCat receives:

  • A random, anonymous app user ID created by Stoke. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Purchase and subscription details from Apple or Google, such as product, price, currency, dates, trial and renewal status, and country of the store account.
  • Basic device and app information, such as operating system, app version, and IP address.

This is needed to provide the Stoke Pro you bought, so the legal basis is performance of a contract. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

International transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process information outside the EEA, including in the United States. These transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long information is kept

  • Information on your device: until you delete it or remove the app.
  • Advertising information: as described in Google's policies.
  • Subscription records: kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide your subscription and to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not hold a separate copy of any of this.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier use.

Because Stoke does not give you an account and we do not store your workouts, most of your information is already under your control on your device. For advertising or subscription information, contact us and we will help with requests to Google or RevenueCat, or contact them directly. How to delete each kind of information is explained at altivia.fi/stoke/delete-data.

If you are in the EEA, you can also complain to a data protection authority. Ours is the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi). You may also contact the authority where you live.

Children

Stoke is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and UK. We do not knowingly allow ads to be personalized for children. If you believe a child has used Stoke in a way that shared personal information, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and publish the new version before the change takes effect in the app. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
