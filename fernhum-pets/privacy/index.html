Fernhum Pets Privacy Policy

Fernhum Pets is published by Altivia Oy (business ID 3548452-7), Finland. Altivia is the data controller for personal information you send us.

Information on your device

Fernhum Pets works without an Altivia account. The app saves these choices and records locally so you can keep using them:

  • Your pet’s name, species, and selected triggers.
  • Your last options for each situation, including duration and background.
  • The chosen training sound, levels for each sound, and a dated history of training reactions.

Altivia does not receive these records. To remove them from your device, delete the app or clear its stored data in your device settings where available. Copies in a device backup are managed through your backup settings.

Device features

Fernhum Pets uses background audio and system playback controls for listening sessions. Android can show a playback notification. Sound Training pauses when you leave the app.

Support and retention

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond and resolve the issue based on our legitimate interest in supporting the app. We keep it as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

Subscriptions

When subscription features are available, Apple or Google handles your payment. We use RevenueCat to check access and restore purchases. It processes an app-generated identifier and information about your store purchases and subscription status to provide your subscription. For details about how RevenueCat handles this information, see its privacy policy.

Your rights and contact

Depending on applicable law, you can ask to access, correct, or erase personal information, restrict or object to its use, or request a copy. Contact Altivia Oy at contact@altivia.fi. You can also complain to your local data protection authority or the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman.
